# Éclat Atelier Studio — design context

## Brand direction

Éclat Atelier Studio should feel elegant, personal and timeless. The visual identity must clearly belong to the same broader Éclat world as Éclat Wine Tour, but the Atelier site must be its own interior-design brand rather than a copy of the Wine Tour website.

Use the reference files in this repository as the basis for design decisions.

## Desired visual character

- editorial rather than template-like;
- refined and premium without looking corporate or overly luxurious;
- warm, tactile, calm and design-led;
- photography and interior details should carry much of the visual weight;
- generous spacing and deliberate composition;
- restrained palette with warm ivory/off-white foundations, dark neutrals and subtle muted-gold accents where appropriate;
- elegant serif/display typography can be paired with a clean supporting sans-serif;
- visual continuity between image and background is welcome: soft masking, gradient fades and blended image edges can be used where they make the composition feel more integrated;
- motion should feel subtle and expensive: slow reveals, image drift/parallax, refined hover states and transitions rather than attention-seeking effects.

## Existing feedback to preserve

Earlier redesign feedback strongly preferred:

- sections where images visually fade/blend into the page background instead of ending in a hard rectangular edge;
- a cleaner, more refined treatment of personal/profile imagery;
- an elegant gold-toned signature element without a visible background box;
- less visual repetition;
- a final implementation that follows the approved visual direction closely, not merely a generic interpretation of it;
- tasteful animation to make the experience feel more exclusive.

## Content and truthfulness constraints

Do not invent or imply any information that has not been supplied in project materials, including:

- references or finished projects;
- customers;
- awards;
- certifications;
- professional credentials;
- services;
- pricing;
- testimonials;
- legal/company information;
- addresses or contact details.

Use clearly marked placeholders for missing data.

## Charles Eames quote

A Charles Eames quotation was requested previously as a possible editorial element. The wording and attribution must be treated as content requiring final approval/verification before publication. Keep it as a placeholder or approved-content item until confirmed.

## Technical direction for the eventual frontend

When implementation begins or resumes, the site should be:

- responsive and mobile-first;
- component-based;
- semantic and keyboard accessible;
- designed with reusable design tokens;
- optimized for image loading and layout stability;
- equipped with a discreet sticky header and a strong mobile navigation experience;
- suitable for a portfolio/project-led structure without forcing every section into a repetitive card grid;
- respectful of `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## What to avoid

Avoid generic SaaS visual language, oversized pill buttons everywhere, excessive rounded cards, loud gradients, neon accents, glassmorphism for its own sake, over-animation, generic stock-luxury styling, and visual effects that compete with the interiors.
